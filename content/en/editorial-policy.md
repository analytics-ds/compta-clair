## Sources used

Our articles rely exclusively on official sources for any legal rule, rate or numerical threshold: service-public.fr, impots.gouv.fr, the official tax bulletin (BOFiP), Urssaf, Légifrance, INSEE, Bpifrance Création and economie.gouv.fr. At least three sources are cited at the bottom of each article, with the date they were accessed.

## Review process

Each article is written by the writer in charge of the topic area, then reviewed before publication to check that the figures used match the cited sources and that explanations remain clear for a non-specialist reader.

## Update frequency

Thresholds, rates and rules change every year, notably after each finance law is passed. Our articles are reviewed at that point, and the last update date is always displayed at the top of the article.

## Correction policy

Any reported error is checked against the corresponding official source and corrected as soon as possible. The article's update date is then revised accordingly.
