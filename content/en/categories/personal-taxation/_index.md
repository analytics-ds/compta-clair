Personal taxation covers income tax, but also more specific schemes tied to rental real estate such as the SCI property company, LMNP status or capital gains tax. This section explains these mechanisms with worked examples.

The articles detail how an SCI works under income tax or corporate tax, the conditions of LMNP status and its depreciation mechanism, and how capital gains tax is calculated when reselling a property.

Content relies on impots.gouv.fr, the official tax bulletin (BOFiP) and Légifrance, updated after every finance law that changes a tax scale or allowance.
