Business accounting rests on precise documents and obligations: chart of accounts, balance sheet, income statement, and now widespread e-invoicing. This section explains these concepts without unnecessary jargon, with concrete examples suited to small businesses.

The articles detail the e-invoicing reform timeline, how to read a balance sheet as a non-specialist manager, and the principles of the general chart of accounts applied to a small company's daily bookkeeping.

The main sources used are the French general chart of accounts, impots.gouv.fr and the official tax bulletin (BOFiP), reviewed after every finance law that changes an accounting or tax rule.
