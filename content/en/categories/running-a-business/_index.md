Running a business day to day means keeping cash flow, expense reports and invoicing under control without spending a disproportionate amount of time on them. This section gathers practical guides to keep these topics in check.

The articles explain how to track a simple cash flow forecast, which expenses are reimbursable through an expense report, and which mentions must appear on every invoice to stay compliant.

Recommendations rely on the French Commercial Code and practical factsheets from economie.gouv.fr, with particular attention to small businesses that handle these topics without an internal accounting department.
