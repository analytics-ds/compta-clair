## Our mission

Les Clés du Dirigeant is an independent media outlet dedicated to accounting, taxation and business creation. Our goal: make topics that are often seen as dry accessible, without ever sacrificing accuracy.

We write for business founders, small business owners, freelancers and individuals looking for a reliable answer to a specific question, whether about a legal structure, a tax deadline or a payslip.

## Editorial team

Our articles are written by a team of specialist writers, each covering a precise topic area. Find their full profiles on the [Authors](/en/authors/) page.

## Working method

Every article primarily relies on official sources: service-public.fr, impots.gouv.fr, the official tax bulletin (BOFiP), Urssaf, Légifrance, INSEE and Bpifrance Création. Figures and thresholds are checked at publication and reviewed after every major regulatory change, notably after each finance law.

Details of our review process and correction policy are available on the [Editorial policy](/en/editorial-policy/) page.

## Independence commitment

Les Clés du Dirigeant receives no compensation from the organizations or businesses mentioned in its articles. When an external link points to a commercial actor, it is identified as such and remains a minority of the published content.
